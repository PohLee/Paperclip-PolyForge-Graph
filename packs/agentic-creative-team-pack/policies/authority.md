# Authority and review

The org chart is separate from approval authority. Product design reports to Design Director; brand work reports to Creative Director; both report to Head of Design. Shared Design Ops supports intake and traceability. The host binds each role to a trusted actor.

Design Director accepts product design specs and compatible product-system changes. Creative Director accepts brand direction and brand-system changes. Head of Design resolves cross-domain tradeoffs, governs shared baselines, and selects concepts only when the user delegates that authority. CPO retains product scope and product acceptance; CTO/BE owners retain technical and API acceptance.

The person who authors a candidate cannot provide its independent review. A separate role name or agent identity is insufficient if the trusted execution identity is the same. User feedback records preference unless the authorized decision path explicitly records approval.
