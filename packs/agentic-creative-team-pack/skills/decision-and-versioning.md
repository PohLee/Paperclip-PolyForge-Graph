# Design decisions and versioning

Every decision names actor identity and role, mode, rule version, exact subjectRefs, evidence, rationale, outcome, and timestamp from the host. A gallery preference is not automatically approval. Preserve rejected options and reconsideration conditions. Changed images, briefs, or baselines get new versions; reports that cite an old digest remain history and cannot approve the replacement.
