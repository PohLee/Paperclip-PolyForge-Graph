# Brand strategy and identity

Work from audience and category evidence toward positioning, differentiation, promise with proof, personality, message hierarchy, and identity rules. Record unsupported claims as open questions. If customer evidence is too weak to support positioning, return `strategy_needs_evidence`; do not treat a territory or identity direction as a substitute for missing evidence. Explore territories only after strategy is stable. Include voice, color, type, graphic language, imagery, composition, and do/don't examples. Concept images do not count as production logo files; identify editable vector and rights checks when requested.
